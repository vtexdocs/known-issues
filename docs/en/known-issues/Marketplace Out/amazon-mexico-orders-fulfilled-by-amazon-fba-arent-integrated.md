## Summary

or stores connected to Amazon Mexico, orders shipped by Amazon itself (Fulfillment by Amazon) are not imported into VTEX. These orders don’t appear in the Orders module or in Bridge, and the merchant gets no error message.
Orders that the merchant ships themselves are still integrated normally.

## Simulation

1. Have a store connected to Amazon Mexico with Fulfillment by Amazon order integration turned on.
2. Receive an order on Amazon that Amazon will ship.
3. Check that the order doesn’t appear in Bridge or in the VTEX Orders module.
