## Summary

In a cart with items added to a gift list, the delivery options and their prices are not always shown to the buyer.
Observed when the user is not logged in.

## Simulation

- Go to the checkout with items from gift list
- Click "Alterar opções de entrega".
- The delivery options are not shown.

## Workaround

There is no workaround available.
